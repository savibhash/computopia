CHAPTER I • THE FIRST PRINCIPLES

Fundamental Pillars of Computation

Before neural networks dream or encrypted packets cross oceans, every computing machine obeys four timeless foundational laws.

🏛️

What is Computer Science?

Computer Science is the formal study of computation, information processing, and algorithmic problem-solving. As the computer scientist Edsger Dijkstra famously noted: "Computer science is no more about computers than astronomy is about telescopes." It is the bridge between abstract mathematics, physical engineering, and human logic.

01 ⚡

Binary & Boolean Logic

At the lowest physical layer, transistors act as microscopic switches representing 0 (Low Voltage) and 1 (High Voltage). Through Boolean gates (AND, OR, NOT, XOR), simple voltage states combine to perform arithmetic, store memory, and execute instructions.

01000011 01010011 = "CS"
02 📐

Algorithms & Data Structures

An Algorithm is a finite, deterministic sequence of instructions designed to solve a class of problems. Data Structures (Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs, Hash Maps) organize data in memory so algorithms can query and mutate it with optimal time and space complexity.

T(n) = O(n log n) • Space: O(1)
03 🖥️

Architecture & Operating Systems

Built on the Von Neumann Architecture (CPU, Memory Unit, I/O, and Control Unit), hardware is orchestrated by the Operating System Kernel (like Linux). The kernel manages CPU scheduling, virtual memory, file systems, and hardware interrupts.

Ring 0: Kernel • Ring 3: User Space
04 🌐

Networks & Distributed Protocols

Computation scales across the globe via packet-switched networks governed by the TCP/IP and OSI models. From physical fiber-optic pulses to DNS resolution, TLS handshakes, and HTTP routing, networks bind isolated machines into one planetary superorganism.

DISPATCH #01 ⏱️ 6 MIN READ ARCHITECTURE

From Babbage’s Brass Gears to 3-Nanometer Silicon: The Evolution of the CPU

In 1837, Charles Babbage conceived the Analytical Engine—a mechanical general-purpose computer programmed with punched cards by Ada Lovelace. Today, billions of FinFET transistors switch billions of times per second inside a single square centimeter of doped silicon. How did humanity compress a room-sized mechanical loom into sub-microscopic quantum tunnels?

#Microarchitecture #AdaLovelace #Semiconductors #MooresLaw
DISPATCH #02 ⏱️ 8 MIN READ CRYPTOGRAPHY

The Asymmetric Miracle: How Two Strangers Share a Secret in Plain Sight

For millennia, encryption required both sender and receiver to secretly meet and exchange a physical key beforehand. In the 1970s, Diffie, Hellman, Rivest, Shamir, and Adleman shattered this limitation using trapdoor one-way mathematical functions—prime factorization and modular arithmetic—making modern HTTPS and SSH possible.

#RSA #PrimeNumbers #ModularArithmetic #ZeroKnowledge
DISPATCH #03 ⏱️ 7 MIN READ ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Matrices That Dream: Inside the Calculus of Deep Neural Networks

Strip away the mystique of modern AI and you find pure multivariable calculus and linear algebra. Every image, word, and sound is embedded as a vector in high-dimensional space, multiplied through weight matrices, and sculpted by the chain rule of derivatives during backpropagation.
